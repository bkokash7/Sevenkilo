Dyograf Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Overview

Dyograf is a personal daily log made by Seven Kilo Enterprises LLC. It is built so that what you write stays with you. Dyograf has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Seven Kilo does not collect, receive, sell or share your information.

Where your information is kept

Everything you put into Dyograf is stored on your device. This includes log entries, events, moods, people and their details, notes, photos, places, voice memos and attached files.

If you are signed in to iCloud, most of it is also stored in your own private iCloud account so it can sync to your other devices. We do not run servers that receive your data, and we have no way to read what is in your iCloud account.

iCloud sync

Sync uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) and is on whenever you are signed in to iCloud. You can turn it off for Dyograf in the Settings app under your name, iCloud. Data in iCloud is protected by Apple and handled under Apple's privacy policy.

Voice memo recordings and files you attach to a person are kept on the device where you added them and do not sync. They are included in backups you create.

Information about other people

Dyograf lets you record details about people in your life, such as names, phone numbers, birthdays, photos and your own notes. This information is stored only as described above and is never sent to us. You decide what to record, and you are responsible for recording it lawfully and respectfully.

Photos and camera

Photos you choose are picked through Apple's photo picker, so Dyograf never has access to the rest of your library. A reduced-size copy is saved inside Dyograf. Location and other details embedded in the original photo are not kept in that copy.

The camera is used only when you take a photo in the app. Photos taken with the in-app camera are also saved to your Photos library at full size. Dyograf can add to your library but cannot read it. You can turn this off in Settings.

To frame photos well, Dyograf looks for faces in a photo on your device. This only finds where a face is. It does not identify anyone, and nothing leaves your device.

Microphone and voice memos

The microphone is used only while you are recording a voice memo. Recordings are turned into text on your device. The audio is not sent to Apple, to us or to anyone else.

Location and maps

Dyograf uses your location only while you are using the app, to centre the map and suggest nearby places when you add a place to an event. It never tracks your location in the background. Places you save are stored with your entries.

Searching for a place or dropping a pin sends the search text or the pin's location to Apple Maps to return results, under Apple's privacy policy. If you tap Open in Google Maps, that place is sent to Google so it can be shown, under Google's privacy policy.

Contacts

If you allow it, Dyograf looks up names in your contacts as you type, to help you add people to an event. Your address book is not copied or uploaded. For a person you add, Dyograf keeps the name, the phone number and a reference to that contact.

When you choose to save a person to your contacts, Dyograf writes only their name, phone number and email. Your notes about them stay in Dyograf.

Calendar

If you allow it, Dyograf reads the events in your calendars to show them in its Calendar tab and in search. They are displayed, not copied into your log. Dyograf adds to your calendar only when you ask it to: a birthday you choose to add, or an event you create and choose to save there.

Notifications

Dyograf can remind you of birthdays and of the intentions you wrote the day before. Reminders are created on your device and are not sent through any server. A reminder can show a person's name or your own words on the lock screen.

App lock

You can lock Dyograf with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. The check is done by iOS. Dyograf never receives your face, fingerprint or passcode.

Backup and export

You can create a backup file (a ZIP archive) and export your log as a PDF. Backups are saved to your iCloud Drive in the Dyograf folder when iCloud Drive is available, and otherwise on your device, where you can find them in the Files app. They are never sent to Seven Kilo.

A backup contains everything in Dyograf and is not encrypted. Anyone who has the file can read it, so keep it somewhere private. Once you share or move a backup or PDF, where it goes is up to you.

Deleting your information

Delete All Data in Settings removes everything Dyograf has stored on your device and in your iCloud account, along with its reminders and the birthday events it added to your calendar. Backup files you saved are yours to keep or delete in the Files app. Deleting the app removes what is stored on that device. You can also remove Dyograf's iCloud data in the Settings app under your name, iCloud, Manage Storage.

Third parties

Dyograf contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. The only outside services involved are Apple's (iCloud, Maps, the App Store) and, only if you tap Open in Google Maps, Google Maps.

Contacting us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply to you.

Children

Dyograf is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and on our website, with a new date at the top.
