Daily log

Dyograf

A record of your days: where you went, who you were with, what happened, and what you want to remember about it. Everything stays on your device unless you decide otherwise.

Coming soon to the App Store
What it does

Daily entries

Write as much or as little as you want, with photographs attached. Entries are dated and stay searchable.

People

Record who was there. Over time you can look back at a person and see everything you did together.

Places

Save the places that recur in your life once, then tag them in a tap instead of retyping an address.

Location picker

Choose a location on a map when you want an entry pinned to somewhere specific.

Voice memos

Record a note when typing isn't practical and keep it attached to the entry.

Calendar view

See your entries laid out by month, and jump to any day.

On This Day

Surfaces what you wrote on the same date in previous years.

Two themes

Warm Light and Modern Dark, chosen to be readable at either end of the day.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

Dyograf holds some of the most personal information you are likely to put in an app. It is built so that information stays yours.

Your journal stays on your device

All content you create, including entries, photographs, places, people, and voice memos, is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that receive it, we do not transmit it anywhere, and we cannot read it.

Permissions the app requests

  • Camera. Requested to allow you to capture photographs directly into an entry.
  • Photo library. Requested to allow you to attach existing photographs to an entry.
  • Location. Requested to enable location tagging in your entries. Location is used only when you tag an entry and is stored with that entry on your device.
  • Microphone. Requested to record voice memos. Recordings are saved on your device.
  • Contacts. Requested to enable the People module, allowing you to add someone from your contacts rather than entering them by hand. The app reads only the contact you select.
  • Calendar. Requested to enable optional calendar integration, so events can appear alongside your entries.

Every permission is optional and can be declined or revoked in your device settings. Declining one disables that feature and nothing else.

Maps

Location features use Apple Maps. When you search for or select a place, that query is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive it.

iCloud sync

Sync is off by default. If you turn it on, your journal is stored in your own private iCloud database under your Apple account, and Apple's terms govern that storage. Even with sync enabled, we have no access to your content.

No tracking, no advertising

  • No analytics services.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party trackers or software development kits.
  • No accounts and no login.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your journal from the device. If iCloud sync was enabled, remove the app's data from your iCloud storage in your device settings as well.
