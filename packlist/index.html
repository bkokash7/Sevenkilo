Packing lists

7K PackList

A packing list for families that tracks which bag each thing went into. Pack item by item, see what is still missing at a glance, and print a label for each bag that shows its contents when scanned.

Coming soon to the App Store
What it does

Trips and travelers

Set up a trip with dates and the people going. Items and bags can belong to one person, so each list stays clear.

Packing by bag

Choose a bag for each item and tap as you pack. Counts show what is done and what is short. On the way home, the list runs in reverse so you can check everything back in.

Item library

About 640 suggestions across clothing, toiletries, documents, tech, and baby gear. Start typing and pick one, or add your own.

Templates

Ten starting lists, such as Beach Holiday and Business Trip, with clothing counts scaled to the length of the trip. Save any trip as your own template.

Before you go

A checklist for the jobs that are not packing: the thermostat, the bins, the water heater. Reorder it and reuse it on every trip.

Bag photos

Photograph each bag from outside and its contents inside. Useful at a lost luggage desk, where a description is hard to give from memory.

Reminders and widget

A reminder a few days before you leave says how much is left to pack and buy. A home screen widget shows progress for the next trip.

Manifests and labels

Print a contents list to fold into a bag, or a QR label for the outside. Scanning the label shows what is inside, with an optional PIN.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

7K PackList keeps your trips on your own devices and in your own iCloud account. The one feature that uses a server of ours is the "Always current" bag label, described below, and that server only ever holds lists it cannot read.

Where your lists are kept

Trips, bags, items, travelers, photos, and checklists are stored on your device and in your own private iCloud database through Apple CloudKit, under your Apple account. Apple's terms govern that storage. We do not receive this data and cannot read it.

Permissions the app requests

  • Camera. Requested when you take a photo of a bag, its contents, or an item.
  • Photo library. The app uses add-only access, so it can save a photo or a printable label to your library but cannot see what is already there. Photos you choose from your library come through the system picker, which shares only the photos you select.
  • Notifications. Requested only after you create a trip with a start date. Reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification server is involved.

Every permission is optional and can be declined or revoked in your device settings. Declining one turns off that feature and nothing else.

Bag labels

The app can make a QR label for a bag. Anyone who scans the label can see the bag's contents list, which is the point of the label, so you choose what goes on it. The owner's name is included unless you turn it off. Trip dates are left out unless you turn them on. You can also require a 4-digit PIN.

There are two kinds of label.

  • Works offline. The list is encrypted and stored inside the code itself. Nothing is sent to us when you make it.
  • Always current. The app encrypts the list on your device and sends only the encrypted copy to our label service at sevenkilo.com. The key needed to read it is kept in the part of the link after the "#" sign, which web browsers do not send to servers, so we cannot read your list. Each list is deleted automatically when the label expires, at most 90 days after you make it, and you can revoke it sooner from the app.

When someone scans a label, the list is decrypted in their browser. The label page sets no cookies and runs no analytics or third-party scripts. Our label service does not log list contents or visitors' IP addresses. It runs on Cloudflare, which handles the network traffic and processes IP addresses to deliver and protect the service under Cloudflare's privacy policy. To limit misuse, the service counts requests using a one-way hash of the visitor's IP address, held briefly in memory and never stored.

Sharing and printing

Manifests, labels, and photos you share or print go through the standard iOS share sheet and print dialog, to the people and services you choose.

No tracking, no advertising

  • No analytics services.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party trackers or software development kits.
  • No accounts and no login.

Deleting your data

Deleting a trip removes it from your devices and your iCloud account. Deleting the app removes your lists from the device. To remove them from iCloud as well, delete the app's data in your device's iCloud settings. To remove an Always current label before it expires, revoke it in the app.
