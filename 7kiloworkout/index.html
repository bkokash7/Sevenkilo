Training log and rep counter

7Kilo Workout

Most training apps want you to stop and type. This one gives you a button large enough to hit without looking, counts what you actually did, and stays out of the way until the set is over.

Coming soon to the App Store
What it does

Buttons you can hit mid-set

The rep control spans the width of the screen. No precision required, no unlocking, no hunting for a small target with tired hands.

Every rep timestamped

Reps are logged as they happen rather than entered as a total afterwards, so the record reflects the set you actually performed.

Sets configured individually

Weight, reps, and rest set per set rather than copied across the whole exercise. Drop sets and pyramids record properly.

Timed holds

For planks, hangs, and anything measured in seconds instead of repetitions.

Distance and carries

Cardio recorded by distance, and loaded carries recorded by both weight and distance.

Rest timer with cues

Counts down with audio cues and a clear visual signal in the final seconds, so you can look away and still know.

Live Activity

The active set and rest timer appear on the lock screen, so the phone stays in your pocket between sets.

Exercise library

Build your own movements and assemble them into workout cards you can start with one tap.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

7Kilo Workout is a personal training log. Your training data belongs to you and stays with you.

What the app stores

Exercises you create, workouts you build, and the sets, repetitions, weights, durations, and distances you record. All of it is stored on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no login, and no server component. We do not receive your training history, and we have no way to see it.

Permissions

  • Notifications. Requested only so the rest timer can alert you when the app is in the background. Declining leaves every other feature working.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photo library, and it does not read Apple Health.

No tracking

  • No analytics or crash reporting services.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party software development kits.
  • The app functions fully without a network connection.

Backup

If you have iCloud backup enabled on your device, your training history is included in that backup under your own Apple account and Apple's terms.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all training history from the device. There is nothing held elsewhere for us to delete.
