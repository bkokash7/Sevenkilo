Seven Kilo Property Services

Property maintenance in Pinellas County.

A property needs steady attention whether or not you have time to give it. We handle maintenance and small repairs across Pinellas County for homeowners, landlords, and owners looking after a place from out of state.

What we do

Maintenance and small repairs

Caulking, weatherstripping, door and cabinet hardware, screens, blinds, shelving, fixture swaps, drywall patching, touch-up paint. The list that never quite gets done.

Preventive maintenance

Filter changes, gutter clearing, pressure washing, exterior caulking, dryer vent cleaning. Small work on a schedule so it doesn't become large work on short notice.

Turnover and make-ready

Getting a unit presentable between tenants, or a house ready to list.

Property checks and condition reports

Scheduled walkthroughs with photographs and a written summary. Useful if you're away, and useful as a dated record either way.

Storm preparation and follow-up

Securing the property ahead of a storm, and documenting afterward for insurance.

Vendor coordination

When something needs a licensed trade, we find one, schedule it, meet them at the property, and confirm the work was done before you pay for it.

Scope of work

We do not perform electrical, plumbing, HVAC, roofing, or structural work, and we do not take jobs that require a permit. When your property needs any of that, we bring in a licensed contractor and oversee the work for you.
