Seven Kilo Press

Books for the kitchen table.

Seven Kilo Press is the publishing imprint of Seven Kilo Enterprises LLC. We make coloring and activity books for kids, maze books, and large print puzzle books for seniors. Every book is printed and sold through Amazon.

Visit sevenkilopress.com
For kids

Coloring and activity books

Realistic coloring books for kids who have outgrown cartoons, big and easy books for the youngest artists, and activity books that follow a story from the first page to the last.

Mazes

Maze books

Full-page mazes in two levels. Find Your Way Out is the relaxing one. No Easy Way Out is for people who want to work for it. Every solution is in the back.

Large print

Puzzle books for seniors

Word searches, sudoku, trivia and brain games in large print, one puzzle per page, with stories and conversation starters about the years many readers grew up in.

Stories

Chapter books

The Four Cats series, written for young readers and published under the author's own name, Ibrahim Kokash.

Questions about a book

Bulk orders for a classroom or senior center, a mistake you found, or an idea for the next book. Orders, shipping and returns are handled by Amazon.
