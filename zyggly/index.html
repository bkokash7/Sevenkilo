Zyggly

Five small games for very young children, built so a parent can hand over the device without watching over their shoulder. No advertising, no purchases, no links out, and no internet connection required.

Download on theApp Store
What it does

Fidget Playground

Things to poke, drag, pop, and spin, with sound and motion that respond immediately to a small hand.

Bubble Pop

Bubbles drift across the screen and burst on contact. Cause and effect, nothing to lose and nothing to fail.

Letter Lab

Letters introduced by shape and sound, at a pace that lets a child sit with one before moving on.

Math Pop

Counting and early number sense through popping, matching, and grouping.

Shape Math

Shapes and simple relationships between them, built into play rather than tested.

Parent gate

Settings sit behind a PIN, so nothing gets changed by accident and there is no way out of the app into somewhere else.

No connection needed

Everything runs on the device. The app works with the network off, which also means nothing leaves it.

Nothing to buy

No advertising, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no upsells anywhere in the app.

Legal

Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

Zyggly is designed for children aged one to five and is published in the App Store Kids Category. This policy explains our approach, which is to collect nothing at all.

We collect no data

Zyggly does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information from anyone, child or adult. There are no accounts, no login, no name or email entry, and no profiles.

No network connection

The app runs entirely on the device and does not require an internet connection to function. No data leaves the device at any point.

No advertising and no purchases

  • No advertising of any kind, including third-party advertising networks.
  • No in-app purchases and no subscriptions.
  • No links out of the app to websites, social media, or other applications.
  • No analytics services, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party software development kits.

Children's privacy

Zyggly is built to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act and with Apple's requirements for apps in the Kids Category. Because the app collects no information whatsoever, there is no personal information about a child for us to hold, use, share, or disclose.

Parent gate

Settings are protected by a PIN entry designed to be operable by an adult and not by a young child, so game selection and preferences cannot be changed accidentally.

What is stored on the device

Only your own settings, such as which games are enabled and the parental PIN. This stays on the device and is removed when the app is deleted.
