Field asset management

7KiloFM

Every asset in one register, on a map, with its full maintenance history attached. Schedule the work before it becomes a breakdown, raise a work order when it doesn't, and pull any asset's record by scanning the code on the side of it.

Coming soon to the App Store
What it does

Asset register

Every piece of equipment recorded once, with make, model, serial, install date, warranty, and the client or site it belongs to.

Location on a map

Place each asset where it physically sits. Useful when a site has forty units and the drawing is ten years out of date.

Scheduled maintenance

Build recurring schedules against an asset and get told what's due rather than finding out afterwards.

Work orders

Raise a job against an asset, assign it, track it to completion, and keep the record attached to that asset permanently.

Projects

Group related work under a project so a refurbishment or a mobilization reads as one thing instead of forty loose jobs.

QR codes

Generate a code for any asset, print it, stick it on. Scanning it opens that asset's full history on the spot.

Client records

Keep sites, contacts, and assets linked, so you can move from a client to their equipment to its last service without searching.

PDF export

Produce a report from any asset, work order, or project and save or send it as a PDF.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

7KiloFM is a working tool. The data you enter is your operational record, and we treat it that way.

What the app stores

Asset records, maintenance schedules, work orders, projects, client and site details, photographs, and any notes you add. This information is created by you and stored on your device.

Permissions the app requests

  • Camera. Requested so you can photograph assets and completed work, and so you can scan QR codes to open an asset record.
  • Location. Requested so you can place an asset at its physical position on a map and find it again later. Location is captured only when you choose to set it.
  • Contacts. Requested only if you choose to import a client or site contact rather than typing it in. The app reads the contact you select and nothing else.
  • Photo library. Requested if you attach an existing photograph to an asset or work order.
  • Files. Requested when you export a report as a PDF, so it can be saved where you choose.

Every permission is optional. Declining one disables that specific feature and nothing else.

What we do not do

  • We do not operate servers that receive your asset data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising identifiers, or third-party trackers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.
  • We have no ability to read your records.

Backup and sync

If you enable iCloud on your device, your records sync through your own Apple account under Apple's terms. We are not party to that transfer and cannot access its contents.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from the device. If you have iCloud sync enabled, remove the app's data from your iCloud storage in your device settings as well.
