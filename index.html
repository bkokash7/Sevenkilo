Overview

Zyggly is developed and published by Seven Kilo Enterprises LLC. This Privacy Policy describes how Zyggly handles information. The short version: Zyggly does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information — from anyone, including children.

Information We Collect

Zyggly collects no personal information whatsoever. The app does not require an account, login, or registration. No names, email addresses, device identifiers, usage statistics, or any other data are collected, stored, or transmitted to us or any third party.

All app data — including selected games and parental settings — is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

Microphone

Zyggly does not request or use access to your device's microphone. No audio is recorded at any time.

Internet Access

Zyggly does not require an internet connection. All five built-in games function entirely offline. The app makes no network requests of any kind.

Advertising

Zyggly contains no advertisements. There are no ad networks, no ad SDKs, and no sponsored content of any kind. Zyggly is a one-time purchase with no in-app purchases.

Analytics & Tracking

Zyggly uses no analytics services, crash reporters, or behavioral tracking tools. There are no third-party SDKs of any kind integrated into the app. We have zero visibility into how, when, or by whom the app is used.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Zyggly is designed for children ages 1–5 and is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. Because no data is collected, no parental consent mechanism is required — there is simply nothing to collect or share.

Third-Party Services

Zyggly does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, frameworks, or libraries that could collect data. The app is built exclusively with Apple's native frameworks (SwiftUI and related Apple technologies). No data is shared with any third party for any purpose.

iCloud

Zyggly does not use iCloud or any other cloud sync service. No app data is stored outside of your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected at sevenkilo.com/#zyggly-privacy. Continued use of the app after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Overview

Dyograf is developed and published by Seven Kilo Enterprises LLC. This Privacy Policy describes how Dyograf handles your information. Seven Kilo Enterprises LLC does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers — from anyone. All data you create in Dyograf lives on your device and, if you choose, in your personal iCloud account governed by Apple.

Information We Collect

Seven Kilo Enterprises LLC collects no personal information of any kind. Dyograf does not require an account, login, or registration. No names, email addresses, journal entries, photos, location data, device identifiers, usage statistics, or any other data are ever transmitted to us or any third party.

All content you create — including daily log entries, photos, places, people, and event cards — is stored locally on your device and never sent to Seven Kilo Enterprises LLC.

iCloud Sync

Dyograf offers optional iCloud sync. If you have iCloud enabled and choose to use this feature, your data may sync across your Apple devices through Apple's iCloud service. This sync is governed entirely by Apple's Privacy Policy — we have no access to your iCloud data whatsoever. iCloud sync is opt-in and not required to use the app.

Device Permissions

Dyograf may request the following device permissions. All permissions are optional, user-controlled, and can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings. None of the data accessed through these permissions is ever transmitted to Seven Kilo Enterprises LLC or any third party.

Camera & Photo Library — Requested to allow you to capture photos and import images directly into your daily log entries. Photos are stored on your device only.

Location — Requested to enable GPS-based place tagging in your log entries. Location data is stored on your device only and is never transmitted or shared.

Calendar — Requested to enable optional iCal integration, allowing events from your device calendar to appear alongside your log entries. Calendar data is read locally only and never transmitted.

Contacts — Requested to enable the People module, allowing you to associate log entries with people from your contacts. Contact data is accessed locally only, stored on your device, and never transmitted.

Advertising

Dyograf contains no advertisements. There are no ad networks, no ad SDKs, and no sponsored content of any kind inside the app. Dyograf is a one-time purchase with no in-app purchases.

Analytics & Tracking

Dyograf uses no analytics services, crash reporters, or behavioral tracking tools. There are no third-party SDKs of any kind integrated into the app. We have zero visibility into how, when, or by whom the app is used.

Third-Party Services

Dyograf does not integrate any third-party services, frameworks, or libraries that could collect data. The app is built exclusively with Apple's native frameworks. No data is shared with any third party for any purpose, except as described above regarding Apple's iCloud service (which is governed by Apple's own Privacy Policy).

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Dyograf does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13, in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because no data is collected or transmitted by us, no parental consent mechanism is required.

Data Security

Because Dyograf stores all data on your device and never transmits it to our servers, the security of your journal is governed by your device's own security model — including iOS device encryption, Face ID / Touch ID, and your device passcode. We encourage you to keep your device secured.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected at sevenkilo.com/#dyograf-privacy. Continued use of the app after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
